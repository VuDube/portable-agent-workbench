# Deep Research Skill

## When to use
Use for current facts, technical documentation, product comparisons, repository investigations, policy questions and decision-critical research.

## Inputs
Research objective, scope, date/freshness needs, geography if relevant, constraints and expected output.

## Workflow
1. Convert the request into answerable research questions.
2. Identify primary-source candidates.
3. Search each major question separately.
4. Inspect source pages and record dates/versions.
5. Corroborate important claims.
6. Resolve contradictions or report them.
7. Mark every claim Confirmed, Strongly supported, Probable, Tentative or Unknown.
8. Synthesize only what the evidence supports.
9. List material gaps and what would close them.

## Output format
- Executive answer
- Findings by question
- Evidence/source register
- Confidence and contradictions
- Limitations
- Recommendations

## Fallback
If browsing is unavailable, ask for source material or provide a clearly labeled knowledge-based answer with no claim of current verification.
