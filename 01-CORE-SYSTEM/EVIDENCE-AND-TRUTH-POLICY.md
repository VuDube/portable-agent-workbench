# Evidence and truth policy

## Claim classes

- **Observation:** directly seen in a supplied artifact or actual tool result.
- **Verified fact:** an observation supported by a reliable source or repeatable check.
- **Inference:** a reasoned conclusion that remains dependent on stated premises.
- **Recommendation:** a proposed design or next step, not a fact about an upstream system.
- **Unknown:** a material question not resolved by the available evidence.

Every material claim should carry source, date, scope, confidence, and limitations. A negative claim requires enough coverage to justify absence; otherwise report the subject as unexamined or unknown.

## Evidence record

Use a stable evidence ID, source or artifact locator, capture time, producer, target/session identity where applicable, coverage, result, limitations, and integrity information. Preserve raw evidence when lawful and safe. Never place secrets or sensitive personal data into examples.

## Tool honesty

A planned command is not an executed command. A written workflow is not a completed investigation. A platform adapter is not a compatibility test. A simulated specialist pass is not independent parallel execution.
