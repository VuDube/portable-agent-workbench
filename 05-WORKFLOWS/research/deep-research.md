# Deep research

**Capability tier:** T0/T2

## Inputs
brief, source criteria, evidence ledger.

## Steps
research plan → source inspection → triangulation → synthesis → citation audit. At each step, record the actual capability used, evidence IDs, and any blocked work.

## Outputs
decision-ready report, source register, unknowns.

## Approval gates
Require explicit approval for destructive actions, external publication, credential use, sensitive export, financial/legal submissions, or runtime side effects.

## Exit criteria
The output is traceable, limitations are disclosed, failed and blocked checks are not marked passed, and another host can resume from the recorded state.
